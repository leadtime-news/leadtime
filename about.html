About Lead Time

Karen Light

Editor and Publisher • Lead Time

Lead Time is an online magazine about thinking ahead: for the parents we help, the children who still need us, and the plans we’re making for ourselves.

The Lead Time website gathers its articles into sections, offers a free home walk-through guide, and sends a free newsletter to subscribers once a week.

Some readers are thinking about their own home. Others are helping a parent think about theirs. Many are doing both at once.

  • Where We Dwell is about the home itself.
  • Distance is about helping a parent from another city.
  • Logistics is about the everyday information a home runs on.
  • Real Talk is about the conversations between generations and between siblings.
  • Sandwiched is for the generation helping a parent while still helping their own children and grandchildren.
  • Next is about your own planning, sometimes prompted by what you are seeing with a parent.

Much of what I write comes from what I observed and learned in my past job as a REALTOR® and Seniors Real Estate Specialist® in Calgary, where many of my clients were in or approaching retirement. That work taught me what to look for in a home, what to ask, and what the answers tend to mean over time. I no longer work as a real estate agent, so that I can focus on Lead Time.

The rest comes from my own life, as the eldest daughter and default sibling supporting living arrangements for a senior parent from a plane ride away. If your situation is similar, I have some idea of what it is like.

That experience is also why I created Kintrak, a family readiness organizer that keeps the everyday answers in one place, so the people closest to you know what to do if someone has to step in on short notice.

Lead Time is for people who would rather decide earlier than later, with plenty of lead time.

In September 2026, the Calgary Citizen ran a sponsored feature on Lead Time and the Kintrak family readiness organizer.
