Free, Available 24/7 and Running on Fumes

Nobody holds a meeting to decide who looks after a parent. It just lands on someone. Care to guess who?

Nobody holds a family meeting to decide who will look after a parent. There is no vote, no sign-up sheet and certainly no job posting. Someone notices the prescription is running low. Someone books the follow-up. Someone remembers which pharmacy fills it, which doctor ordered it and which day the refill is due.

Congratulations. Someone just got a second job. No salary, no benefits, no vacation.

More often than not, that someone is a woman.

If that sounds familiar, you may have already seen the proof. In November 2026, Saturday Night Live aired a fake commercial, with John Mulaney as host, for an elder care service called “Sister”, pitched as “a live-in, 24/7 elder care provider that is completely free, and also your sister”. It was funny because it was true. It was painful for exactly the same reason.
