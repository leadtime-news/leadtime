Privacy

Lead Time is published by Raeven Logic Systems Inc. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

When you subscribe to Lead Time, we collect your email address. If you give us a first name, we collect that too. We don't ask for anything else.

What we do with it

We use your email address to send you Lead Time. Your walk-through guide downloads straight away when you sign up, and our welcome email includes a link to it so you can find it again later. That's it. We don't sell your information, rent it, trade it, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

Who else sees it

Three services help us run Lead Time:

  • beehiiv stores subscriber email addresses and sends our emails. Their privacy practices are described at beehiiv.com/privacy.
  • Mailchimp was our previous email service and still holds a copy of subscriber email addresses while we finish moving away from it. Their privacy practices are described at mailchimp.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google Analytics provides the site analytics. Their practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Both companies are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable law. We don't share data with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

The site uses cookies for Google Analytics. If you'd rather not be tracked, most browsers let you block cookies in their settings, and Google offers a browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout that opts you out across all sites.

Unsubscribing and your data

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're out.

If you want us to delete your information entirely from our records, email editor@leadtime.news and we'll take care of it.
