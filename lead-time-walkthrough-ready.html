Some of the questions families mean to ask, the ones that never seem to find the right moment, can be answered and set down gently in one place.

This is partly why I created a family readiness organizer called Kintrak.

It gathers what a family would need to know if someone had to step in to help on short notice:

  • the first calls to the people involved
  • how the home works and who services what
  • health basics
  • how pets, if any, are cared for
  • where the important paperwork is kept
  • and the routines that keep an ordinary day running

Things go more easily when information is written down now, while there is no reason to hurry.
